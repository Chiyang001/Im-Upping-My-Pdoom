# v20 camera and upgrade preview review

The integrated production preview passes the focused visual review after the
closer camera, apparatus exposure and exit-bridge refinements.

The 25 actual 1920×1080 frames span 127.80–132.40 seconds. All three contact
pages were inspected, with native-size checks at 128.470, 129.420, 130.360 and
132.200 seconds. These are rendered production frames rather than geometry
mockups. The old central concentric-ring presentation is absent from the new
archive / training / extraction / fabrication sequence.

- At **masked**, opposed shutters and the moving archive feed share a fixed
  machine housing. The lyric occupies its physical mounting frame.
- At **pre-training / days**, the camera cuts across the training apparatus;
  the comb, sample path and indexing chain move with the sung cues. Floor,
  foreground machinery and distant supports have distinct parallax.
- At **to / recursive**, the camera rises with extraction and crosses the
  fabrication front. The word planes retain their relation to the machinery.
- At **self-upgrade**, the scene hands off to the existing forward word flight
  through successive deeper fabrication structures. The original lyric pass
  supplies the acceleration and impact.
- An initially almost empty frame at **132.200** was found during review.
  Retaining the successor structure and adding a fixed physical bridge leaves
  visible foreground trusses and rails through that fast exit. The revised
  native frame was inspected and the finding is resolved.

The actual camera diagnostic samples **225 words in 166 lexical groups** at
30 fps. It removes the common forward world travel and sets the transient hit
shake to zero. All **166 groups** have observed local translation, direction,
bank, lens or pair-boundary change under the published review thresholds. No
invalid camera directions or singular/non-finite projection matrices were
found. The equivalent v19 camera-only baseline had 146 covered groups and 20
groups requiring additional scene-action or visual review.

This is evidence of camera activity, not a mathematical measure of beauty or
word meaning. Director names and phase counters do not establish coverage.
The separate full spatial-word audit determines whether the moving physical
letters are readable; a frame can be energetic while its words are clipped.
