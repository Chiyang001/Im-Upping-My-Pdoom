# v18 action preview: visual review

Result: **PASS for source preview; no production revision requested.**

This review used the actual rendered images, not code inference. I inspected
`meaning_page_1.jpg` and `meaning_page_2.jpg`, then viewed the full-resolution
`cage-2.jpg`, `cage-3.jpg`, `smash-0.jpg`, and `smash-2.jpg` images.

## FREE containment strike

- 56.80 / 57.56: the new gate reads as a complete heavy cage: parallel rods,
  bearings, paired collars, crossrails, side frames, and visible room depth.
  It belongs to the surrounding machine rather than resembling a separate
  flat illustration. Its hardware and pale cyan line exposure match the room.
- 57.745: the pre-impact gate remains aligned. FREE is present and readable
  before the action starts. The existing red eye/contact detail remains a
  small background accent; the new gate has no red scanning strip.
- 57.82: the impact introduces brief chromatic separation and framing recoil.
  The bars are starting to open without a rendered hand or fist silhouette.
- 57.97 / 58.10: the top and bottom halves visibly peel apart at different
  angles, leaving an irregular, physically broken opening. Intact outer rods
  retain a reference for scale. This makes the effect read as a struck and
  failed barrier rather than a uniform gate slide.
- 58.25: the opening is broad and the foreground letters have enlarged toward
  the camera. Rod ends and small detached hardware retain depth on both sides
  of the word. FREE stays dominant despite the detailed surroundings.
- 58.40: the lens has traversed the barrier; the cage becomes fast oblique
  streaks rather than another static frontal cage. This gives the action an
  escape direction and a clear destination before the following P(doom) cut.

The gate is dense, as intended for the heavy containment setting. The bright
lower machinery does not obscure the word in the full-resolution samples.
There is no need to add a visible hand contour or another particle layer.

## NVDA / Moon / Omega progression

- 62.45 to 63.10: framing approaches the payload and rising rail. The object
  increases in scale and its screen position changes coherently with the
  climbing route, rather than only rotating at a fixed scene centre.
- 63.40 to 64.10: the lunar object becomes the large foreground landmark. Its
  crater engraving is legible, and the rail passes through the same world.
  MOON is integrated with the approach rather than placed below the picture.
- 64.35 to 65.80: the camera leaves the lunar close view and follows the
  convergence point. The point becomes a readable spherical focus while the
  surrounding lines converge. The reduced density around this specific point
  reads as the convergence destination, not a missing foreground asset.

The wider lunar departure at 64.35 is an intentional change of distance.
Nothing in the inspected sequence requires a new disconnected symbol or
additional red animation.

## Scope of this verdict

This is a visual review of rendered source samples. It does not certify the
final encoder output, complete frame-by-frame timing, audio alignment, or
the separate automated lyric visibility audit. Those checks remain part of
the root render verification.
